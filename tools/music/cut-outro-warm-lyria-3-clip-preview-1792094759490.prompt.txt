Understated, playful victory music for a short animated cut scene, the same style as a sneaky night-time cartoon heist score: three house cats have just chased the raccoon burglars out the window, the house is safe again and the night light glows. A bouncy pizzicato string melody turned major and cheeky, sly clarinet and bassoon playing a jaunty little tune, glassy celesta and music-box twinkles, soft marimba, light brushed snare and finger snaps, a warm low cello pad, a couple of gentle muted-trumpet stings. Smug and relieved, cosy and mischievous rather than grand. No big brass fanfare, no cymbal crashes, no marching band. About 110 BPM, major key, same sound world as the heist music so it feels like the same score resolving. Instrumental, no vocals. Start immediately with the pizzicato tune.
