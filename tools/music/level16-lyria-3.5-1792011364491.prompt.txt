Playful, mischievous instrumental cartoon music for a casual mobile slingshot game about three house cats chasing raccoon burglars who stole their treats. Bouncy pizzicato strings, marimba, light ukulele, soft brushed drums and a cheeky clarinet or bassoon melody, like a heist caper for cats. Bright, happy, a little sneaky. Around 110 BPM, steady tempo, major key. No vocals. Seamless loop: no intro, no fade-in or fade-out, and it ends right where it began so it can repeat endlessly. Level flavour: lights out, a stealthy heist in the dark with the lamps flicking on one by one. Hushed and sneaky: plucked pizzicato and a soft fingerstyle bass tiptoeing, a glassy celesta and music-box twinkle for each lamp switching on, low muted brass and a sly bass clarinet melody, a slow shimmering moonlit pad, sparse brushed drums with finger snaps. Night-time, a touch mysterious, still playful.
